About North Star Bakery

Our story, our ingredients, and our people.

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on creating fresh breads, pastries, and cakes for our local community.

Our goal is to create a welcoming place where customers can enjoy freshly baked products made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that good baking starts with quality ingredients. We focus on thoughtful ingredient choices and careful preparation to create products our customers can enjoy.

Our Staff

Our bakery team works together to prepare, bake, and serve our products each day. We are proud to provide friendly service to our neighborhood customers.
